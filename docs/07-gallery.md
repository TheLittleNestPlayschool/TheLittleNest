# Gallery

The Gallery is not simply a place to store photos and videos.

It is the family's visual history of their child's journey through The Little Nest.

Every visit should remind parents how much their child has grown.

The Gallery should become more valuable with time.

---

# Purpose

The Gallery allows parents to revisit meaningful moments throughout their child's learning journey.

It brings together photographs, videos, celebrations, achievements, and replayable memories into a single experience.

Parents should feel they are browsing their child's story rather than searching through files.

---

# Living Memories

The Gallery is built around the idea of Living Memories.

A Living Memory may include:

- Photos
- Videos
- Badge celebrations
- Milestone celebrations
- Character interactions
- Seasonal events
- Classroom activities
- Recognition moments
- Special announcements
- Replayable celebrations from Memory Nest

Every meaningful event should have the opportunity to become a lasting memory.

---

# Timeline

Memories should naturally organize themselves over time.

Examples:

- Today
- This Week
- This Month
- This Year

Eventually the Gallery should allow parents to revisit any period of their child's journey.

The experience should feel like looking through a family scrapbook that grows automatically.

---

# Memory Nest Integration

Important milestones should automatically appear in the Gallery.

When a celebration is preserved in Memory Nest, parents should also be able to find it while browsing the Gallery timeline.

The Gallery and Memory Nest work together to tell the complete story of the child's development.

---

# Parent Experience

Parents should regularly discover moments they had forgotten.

A simple classroom photo may become one of their favourite memories years later.

The Gallery should encourage parents to pause, smile, remember, and share those experiences with the people they love.

---

# Design Intent

The Gallery should become one of the most valuable parts of the application.

Years after leaving The Little Nest, parents should still enjoy opening the Gallery to relive their child's early years.

The goal is not simply to preserve media.

The goal is to preserve childhood.

---

# Future Expansion

Potential future enhancements include:

- Search by date
- Search by activity
- Search by learning category
- Character appearances
- Seasonal collections
- Automatic "On This Day" memories
- Favourite memories
- Shared family collections
- Downloadable memory books
- Printed keepsake albums
- AI-generated yearly highlight reels
