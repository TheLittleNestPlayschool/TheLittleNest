# Purpose

The Experience Engine transforms classroom moments into meaningful family experiences.

It does not simply display data.

It understands context.

It selects meaningful ingredients.

It composes experiences.

It prepares the Living Stage.

The Experience Engine exists so parents experience childhood rather than information.

---

# Philosophy

Every family opens The Little Nest for a different reason.

Some want to discover today's story.

Some want to replay a favourite memory.

Some want to celebrate.

Some simply want to feel connected.

The Experience Engine should understand these possibilities before presenting an experience.

The goal is never to personalise for the sake of personalisation.

The goal is to understand what matters most right now.

---

# The Experience Pipeline

Every experience follows the same journey.

Moments occur.

↓

Teachers capture meaningful observations.

↓

Automatic systems collect supporting information.

↓

Experience Ingredients are created.

↓

Editorial Philosophy determines what matters most.

↓

The Experience Engine assembles the experience.

↓

The Living Stage presents it.

↓

The family experiences it.

↓

Replay creates new memories.

---

# Sources Of Understanding

The Experience Engine builds understanding from many sources.

These include:

Classroom information.

Learning progress.

Teacher observations.

Photographs.

Videos.

Previous experiences.

Memory Nest.

Story Reel.

Schedule.

Future lessons.

Parent interaction history.

No single source should determine the experience.

Meaning comes from combining many small signals.

---

# Context

Context changes everything.

The Experience Engine should understand questions such as:

Has today's experience already been viewed?

Has today's Story Reel been watched?

Did the child earn a badge?

Is today a birthday?

Is tomorrow Recognition Day?

Has the parent missed several classes?

Is there an "On This Day" memory?

Has the parent replayed today's experience several times?

Every decision should consider the family's current context.

---

# Parent Behaviour

The Experience Engine should learn from behaviour without restricting choice.

Examples include:

Last login.

Favourite experiences.

Replay history.

Sharing history.

Completed experiences.

Unfinished experiences.

Frequently revisited memories.

Behaviour provides context.

It should never remove the parent's freedom to explore.

---

# Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is one tool within the Experience Engine.

Its purpose is not to invent stories.

It is not to replace teachers.

It is not to manipulate emotion.

Its purpose is to help organise meaningful ingredients into experiences that feel natural, personal, and authentic.

AI supports editorial decisions.

It does not replace editorial philosophy.

---

# Rules

Some decisions should always follow clear rules.

Examples include:

Birthdays.

Recognition Days.

Critical announcements.

Safety information.

Upcoming classes.

Mandatory experiences should always take priority over optional experiences.

Rules create consistency.

---

# Editorial

The Experience Engine always follows the Editorial Philosophy.

When several meaningful experiences are possible, it asks:

Which experience will create the strongest connection?

Which experience will create the greatest conversation?

Which experience will families remember?

Meaning always wins.

---

# Composition

Once the opening experience has been selected, the Experience Engine chooses the building blocks needed to compose it.

Some experiences require:

One photograph.

One observation.

Others may include:

Story Reel.

Gallery.

Teacher Reflection.

Conversation Starter.

Learning Journey.

Tomorrow's Adventure.

Every experience should feel complete without feeling crowded.

---

# Learning Over Time

The Experience Engine should improve over time.

Not because it collects more data.

Because it better understands what creates meaningful family experiences.

Future improvements should make experiences more thoughtful.

Never more complicated.

---

# Transparency

Parents should always trust what they experience.

The Experience Engine should never exaggerate.

Never invent.

Never create false memories.

Never imply something that did not happen.

Authenticity is more important than excitement.

---

# Success

The Experience Engine succeeds when families feel:

"This felt made for us."

Without ever wondering how it was created.

The technology should quietly disappear.

Only the experience should remain.

---

# Final Principle

The Experience Engine should never ask:

"What can we show?"

It should always ask:

**"What experience will help this family feel closest to their child right now?"**

When every decision begins with that question...

Technology becomes invisible.

Childhood becomes unforgettable.
