# Purpose

Experience Ingredients define everything The Little Nest knows that may contribute to creating a meaningful family experience.

These ingredients are not shown directly to parents.

They are the raw materials from which experiences are created.

Just as ingredients become a meal...

Moments become memories.

The purpose of collecting ingredients is never to present more information.

The purpose is to create better experiences.

---

# Philosophy

Parents do not experience database records.

They experience stories.

They experience celebrations.

They experience memories.

A photograph is not an experience.

A badge is not an experience.

A teacher observation is not an experience.

Attendance is not an experience.

An API response is not an experience.

They are ingredients.

The Living Stage combines these ingredients into meaningful experiences that families will remember.

---

# Every Ingredient Matters

Some ingredients are large.

A Recognition Day.

A Birthday.

A First Badge.

Others are almost invisible.

A smile.

Helping a friend.

Trying again.

A thoughtful question.

Sometimes the smallest ingredient creates the most meaningful experience.

Nothing should be considered too small to matter.

---

# Categories Of Ingredients

Every ingredient belongs to one or more categories.

These categories help the Experience Engine understand what is available when composing an experience.

---

# Classroom Ingredients

These describe what happened during the child's session.

Examples include:

- Attendance
- Session completed
- Arrival time
- Departure time
- Classroom
- Teacher
- Activities completed
- Special classroom events
- Visitors
- Seasonal activities
- Free play
- Outdoor play
- Music
- Story time
- Creative activities
- Experiments
- Games

These ingredients provide context for today's experience.

---

# Learning Ingredients

These describe the child's learning journey.

Examples include:

- Learning Path progress
- Session objectives
- Skills introduced
- Skills practised
- Nine learning category scores
- Badges earned
- Milestones reached
- Areas requiring encouragement
- Areas showing confidence
- Next lesson
- Future learning path

Learning ingredients explain growth.

They should never replace the child's story.

---

- Celebrations shared

# Future Ingredients

The future is part of today's experience.

- Tomorrow's lesson
- Upcoming activity

---

# Ingredients Become Experiences

Experience Ingredients are never shown directly.

Instead, they are selected, combined, and arranged into meaningful experiences.

Some experiences may require only:

- One photograph.
- One teacher observation.

Others may combine:

- Photos
- Videos
- Teacher reflections
- Learning progress
- Story Reel
- Memory Nest
- Celebrations
- Future adventures

Quality is always more important than quantity.

The best experiences are rarely the ones with the most ingredients.

They are the ones with the right ingredients.

---

# Growing Over Time

The list of ingredients will continue to expand.

Future versions of The Little Nest may include new sources of meaningful moments.

New ingredients should only be added if they strengthen the family experience.

More information does not automatically create better experiences.

Better ingredients create better experiences.

---

# Final Principle

Experience Ingredients exist to help The Little Nest understand a child's story.

They are never the destination.

They are the beginning.

When thoughtfully combined, ordinary classroom moments become extraordinary family experiences.

That is the purpose of every ingredient collected by The Little Nest.
