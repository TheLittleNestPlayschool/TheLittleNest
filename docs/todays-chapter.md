# Purpose

Today's Chapter exists to reconnect parents with their child's day.

It is not a daily report.

It is not an attendance summary.

It is not a collection of classroom data.

It is the first chapter parents open when they return to The Little Nest.

Its purpose is to help parents feel that they experienced part of their child's day, even if they were not there.

If parents close Today's Chapter feeling closer to their child than when they opened it, Today's Chapter has fulfilled its purpose.

---

# Why Today's Chapter Exists

Parents spend much of the day apart from their children.

Teachers witness moments that parents never see.

Some of those moments are educational.

Many are not.

A first smile.

Helping a friend.

Trying again after struggling.

Asking a thoughtful question.

Laughing during story time.

Waiting patiently.

These moments are often forgotten.

Today's Chapter exists to preserve the feeling of the day before it becomes just another memory.

---

# Design Philosophy

Parents are not opening an application.

They are opening today's chapter in their child's story.

Every day should feel different because every child is different.

Some chapters will be exciting.

Some will be quiet.

Some will be funny.

Some will be deeply meaningful.

Not every chapter needs a major achievement.

Every chapter should contain something worth remembering.

# Teacher Philosophy

Teachers should never feel responsible for writing beautiful stories.

Teachers create beautiful moments.

The application helps tell those stories.

The teacher's responsibility is simply to notice.

The software assembles those observations into Today's Chapter.

Technology exists to reduce workload, not increase it.

---

# Teacher Experience

Teachers are busy.

Children come first.

Administrative work should remain minimal.

Recording Today's Chapter should feel like capturing memories, not writing reports.

Whenever possible:

Attendance should already be known.

Completed session should already be known.

Learning progress should already be known.

Next lesson should already be known.

Teachers should only record the moments that software cannot observe.

---

# AI Philosophy

Artificial Intelligence should never invent memories.

It should never exaggerate.

It should never replace the teacher's observations.

Instead, AI should help organise meaningful moments into a story that feels natural, personal, and easy for parents to experience.

AI should answer one question:

**"Out of everything that happened today, what would this family most want to remember?"**

---

# Parent Conversation

Every Today's Chapter should encourage a conversation after school.

Parents should naturally want to ask questions such as:

"What was your favourite part today?"

"Tell me about your butterfly."

"Who did you help today?"

"What made you laugh?"

"What happened next?"

The conversation is more important than the screen.

# Relationship With Story Reel

Story Reel lets parents experience the day.

Today's Chapter helps parents understand the day.

Story Reel is immersive.

Today's Chapter is reflective.

Together they tell today's complete story.

---

# Relationship With Memory Nest

Today's Chapter is temporary.

Memory Nest is permanent.

Most chapters will simply be enjoyed.

Some chapters will become treasured family memories.

The most meaningful moments should eventually become part of Memory Nest.

Today's Chapter writes today's story.

Memory Nest preserves it forever.

---

# Success Is Not Measured By...

How many words were written.

How many photos were uploaded.

How many badges were earned.

How many activities were completed.

Success is measured by one question:

**Did this help a parent feel closer to their child today?**
