# Purpose

This document defines the storytelling building blocks that can be used to compose any parent experience within The Little Nest.

It does not define the interface.

It does not define implementation.

It does not define how experiences are selected.

Instead, it defines the pieces available to create meaningful experiences.

Some experiences may use only a few building blocks.

Others may use many.

The goal is never to show everything.

The goal is to create an experience families will remember.

---

# Design Philosophy

Every experience should feel like someone who knows the child is saying,

> "There's something I'd love to share with you."

Sometimes that story is exciting.

Sometimes it is quiet.

Sometimes it celebrates.

Sometimes it remembers.

Every experience should feel natural, personal, and authentic.

---

# Composition Philosophy

Experiences are assembled from meaningful moments.

Not reports.

Not statistics.

Not database records.

Every experience should focus on what matters most.

Quality is always more important than quantity.

---

# Experience Building Blocks

The following storytelling elements are available when composing experiences throughout The Little Nest.

No experience is expected to use every building block.

Different experiences naturally use different combinations.

---

# 1. Welcome

Purpose

Welcome the family back.

Examples

- Welcome back.
- Good afternoon.
- Happy Birthday.
- Happy Recognition Day.
- Welcome to today's adventure.

Usually presented by Tita Mayumi or Pico.

---

# 2. Hero Moment

Purpose

Present the single most meaningful moment.

Every experience should have one emotional centre.

Examples

- Helped another child.
- Earned a badge.
- Became more confident.
- Tried something difficult.
- Shared for the first time.

---

# 3. Story

Purpose

Help parents understand what happened.

Stories explain moments.

Not statistics.

---

# 4. Teacher Reflection

Purpose

Share something only a teacher could notice.

Observations.

Encouragement.

Kindness.

Growth.

Never evaluations.

---

# 5. Learning

Purpose

Connect the experience to the child's learning journey.

Learning should support the story.

Never replace it.

---

# 6. Discovery

Purpose

Highlight something new.

A new idea.

A new skill.

A new friendship.

A new question.

---

# 7. Character

Purpose

Celebrate confidence, kindness, courage, curiosity and resilience.

Growth matters as much as achievement.

---

# 8. Gallery Moment

Purpose

Use a meaningful photograph or short video to strengthen the story.

The most meaningful image is not always the best photograph.

---

# 9. Story Reel

Purpose

Invite parents into a more immersive experience.

Today's story explains.

Story Reel lets families experience it.

---

# 10. Conversation

Purpose

Encourage conversations after the phone is put away.

The greatest success happens at home.

---

# 11. Looking Forward

Purpose

Create anticipation.

Tomorrow's adventure.

The next milestone.

An upcoming celebration.

Learning should always continue.

---

# 12. Celebration

Purpose

Recognise something worth celebrating.

Badges.

Birthdays.

Recognition Days.

Milestones.

Achievements.

Acts of kindness.

---

# 13. Replay

Purpose

Invite families to revisit meaningful moments.

Replay should strengthen relationships.

Not simply repeat content.

---

# 14. Memory

Purpose

Occasionally recognise that today's experience deserves to become part of Memory Nest.

This should happen sparingly.

Meaning creates memories.

Not frequency.

---

# Character Participation

## Tita Mayumi

Welcomes.

Guides.

Explains.

Encourages.

Celebrates.

She gently leads families into each experience.

---

## Pico

Explores.

Discovers.

Celebrates.

Points.

Laughs.

Asks questions.

He experiences the journey alongside the child.

---

# Composition Guidelines

Every experience should feel:

Warm.

Natural.

Hopeful.

Personal.

Simple.

Authentic.

Never rushed.

Never overloaded.

Parents should always leave wanting one more moment.

---

# Final Principle

Experiences are not composed to show everything that happened.

They are composed to show what mattered most.

If a family closes an experience feeling closer to one another than when they opened it...

Then it has been beautifully composed.
