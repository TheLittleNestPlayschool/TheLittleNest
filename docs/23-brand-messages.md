# Purpose

This document captures memorable phrases that express the philosophy, vision, culture, and emotional experience of The Little Nest.

These are not advertising slogans.

They are the language of our organization.

Some may appear in marketing.

Some may appear inside the app.

Some may be spoken by teachers.

Some may become part of our culture.

Every message should reflect who we are and what we believe.

---

# Internal Culture

We don't build software.

We build experiences.

We don't teach buttons.

We teach purpose.

We don't create features.

We create moments.

Technology serves families.

People always come first.

Every decision should strengthen the connection between parent and child.

Every feature should create an emotion.

Every interaction should add value.

Before we teach people how we operate...

We teach them who we are.

Features create moments.

Moments create memories.

Memories create lifelong relationships.

Software supports the experience.

People create the experience.

Culture is our greatest feature.
