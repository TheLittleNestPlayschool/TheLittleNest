# Brand Messages

**Status:** Living Document
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-31
**Owner:** Dave Stoyko

**Purpose**

This document captures memorable phrases that express the philosophy, vision, culture, and emotional experience of The Little Nest.

These are not advertising slogans.

They are the language of our organization.

Some may appear in marketing.

Some may appear inside the app.

Some may be spoken by teachers.

Some may become part of our culture.

---

# Growth

Today I am a little better than I was yesterday.

Every child grows at their own pace.

Growth deserves celebration.

Learning never ends.

Small steps become big journeys.

Every day is another chapter.

Progress is more important than perfection.

Celebrate effort.

Celebrate curiosity.

Celebrate growth.

# Parents

Every child has a story.

Every parent deserves to see it.

Your child did something wonderful today.

See your child's day through their eyes.

Because childhood only happens once.

Today's memories become tomorrow's stories.

Open another chapter.

# Families

# Learning

School teaches children how to learn.

Learning teaches us how to live.

Curiosity is the beginning of learning.

Learning is a lifelong journey.

# Internal Culture

We don't build software.

We build experiences.

We teach purpose.

Technology serves families.

People always come first.

Every decision should strengthen the connection between parent and child.
