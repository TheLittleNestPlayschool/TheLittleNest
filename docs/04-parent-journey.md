# Parent Journey

The Parent App is not a collection of screens.

It is an ongoing story that allows parents to experience, celebrate, and participate in their child's development.

Every interaction should strengthen the connection between parent and child.

The goal is for parents to look forward to opening the application because they know there is always something new to discover.

---

# The Journey

## Stage 1 - Curiosity

A parent enrolls their child.

They are naturally curious.

Questions include:

- What will my child learn?
- Will they enjoy it?
- Are they making friends?
- Did I choose the right playschool?

The application should immediately begin answering those questions.

---

## Stage 2 - Discovery

Parents begin receiving photos, videos, progress updates, and small achievements.

They begin to understand how their child learns through play.

The application starts replacing uncertainty with confidence.

---

## Stage 3 - Connection

Parents begin checking the application regularly.

Not because they have to.

Because they enjoy seeing their child's progress.

Characters, animations, notifications, and celebrations create an emotional connection with the application.

---

## Stage 4 - Pride

Parents begin celebrating achievements.

Badges.

Progress.

Photos.

Videos.

Learning milestones.

These become moments they naturally want to share with spouses, grandparents, relatives, and friends.

The application should create opportunities for parents to celebrate their child's accomplishments.

---

## Stage 5 - Anticipation

Parents begin looking forward to what comes next.

Another badge.

Another milestone.

Another story.

Another surprise.

Learning never feels finished.

The next adventure is always waiting.

---

## Stage 6 - Loyalty

The application has become part of the family's routine.

Parents no longer see The Little Nest as simply a playschool.

It has become a trusted partner in their child's development.

---

# Emotional Goals

Parents should regularly experience:

- Curiosity
- Confidence
- Joy
- Pride
- Surprise
- Encouragement
- Hope
- Excitement

Fear, confusion, frustration, and disappointment should be minimized wherever possible.

---

# Sharing Moments

Parents naturally enjoy celebrating their children's achievements.

The application should create authentic moments that parents are proud to share with family and friends.

Every shared moment strengthens both the family relationship and the Little Nest community.

---

# Continuous Growth

There is no finish line.

Learning is an ongoing journey.

Badges lead to new badges.

Milestones lead to new milestones.

Achievements lead to new adventures.

The application should never communicate that learning is complete.

Every celebration is the beginning of the next chapter.

---

# Our Goal

When a parent opens The Little Nest, they should never feel like they are checking school records.

They should feel like they are opening the next chapter in their child's story.

Every child has a story.

Every parent deserves to see it.
