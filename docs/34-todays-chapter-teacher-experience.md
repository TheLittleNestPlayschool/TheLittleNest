# Purpose

Teacher Capture defines how teachers contribute meaningful moments to The Little Nest experience.

Teachers are not responsible for creating stories.

Teachers are responsible for noticing moments.

The Experience Engine transforms those moments into experiences families will remember.

The goal is to reduce administration while increasing meaning.

---

# Philosophy

Children come first.

Always.

Capturing experiences should never interfere with teaching.

Every additional tap must justify its value.

If information can be collected automatically, teachers should never have to enter it manually.

Teachers should spend their time creating moments.

Not documenting them.

---

# Teachers Create Moments

Teachers create:

Belonging.

Confidence.

Curiosity.

Kindness.

Friendship.

Growth.

Joy.

The application should never ask teachers to recreate those moments later.

Instead, it should simply help them preserve the moments worth remembering.

---

# Capture, Don't Write

Teachers should never feel they are writing reports.

Or composing stories.

Or filling in paperwork.

They simply capture meaningful observations.

A few words.

A photograph.

A short note.

A quick selection.

The software does the rest.

---

# What The System Already Knows

Teachers should never be asked for information the system already has.

Examples include:

Attendance.

Class schedule.

Session completed.

Learning objectives.

Learning Path progress.

Activities completed.

Teacher assigned.

Classroom.

Student information.

Future lesson.

These should already exist before Teacher Capture begins.

---

# What Only Teachers Know

Some moments can never be measured automatically.

Only teachers can notice them.

Examples include:

A first smile.

Helping another child.

Trying again.

Showing kindness.

Being brave.

Making everyone laugh.

An unexpected question.

Growing in confidence.

A memorable quote.

A beautiful act of empathy.

These observations often become the emotional heart of the family's experience.

---

# Teacher Reflections

Occasionally a teacher may wish to share something more personal.

Examples include:

"I was proud of Emma today."

"She showed wonderful curiosity."

"I noticed how patiently she waited."

These reflections should remain short.

Authentic.

Natural.

Human.

Parents value genuine observations more than polished writing.

---

# Photographs

Photographs should capture real moments.

Not staged moments.

Not posed moments.

Children learning.

Playing.

Creating.

Helping.

Discovering.

Laughing.

Natural moments tell the strongest stories.

---

# Videos

Short videos should capture moments that photographs cannot.

Movement.

Laughter.

Music.

Celebrations.

Curiosity.

Videos do not need to be perfect.

Authenticity matters more than production quality.

---

# Teacher Workload

Teacher Capture should require as little effort as possible.

Whenever practical:

One tap is better than two.

Automatic is better than manual.

Selection is better than typing.

Observation is better than reporting.

The teacher should always feel that the software is helping them rather than slowing them down.

---

# Trust The Experience Engine

Teachers should not worry about creating the perfect story.

They should trust the Experience Engine to combine their observations with everything else already known.

The richest experiences come from combining many small ingredients together.

The teacher contributes only the ingredients that software cannot observe.

---

# Less Is Enough

Not every child needs a long note.

Not every class needs multiple photographs.

Not every session creates a major milestone.

One meaningful observation is often enough.

One wonderful photograph may become a treasured family memory.

Quality always matters more than quantity.

---

# Consistency

Teacher Capture should feel the same in every Little Nest.

Simple.

Friendly.

Quick.

Encouraging.

Teachers should quickly develop confidence that every meaningful observation they record has the potential to become a cherished family experience.

---

# Future Possibilities

Teacher Capture may continue to evolve.

Possible future enhancements include:

Voice notes.

Photo annotation.

Quick emotion selection.

Suggested observations.

AI-assisted categorisation.

Offline capture.

Collaborative observations.

Smart reminders.

Future improvements should always reduce workload rather than increase it.

---

# Final Principle

Teachers should leave each class feeling they spent their time teaching children.

Not completing administration.

If Teacher Capture allows teachers to preserve meaningful moments in just a few simple actions...

Then it has fulfilled its purpose.
