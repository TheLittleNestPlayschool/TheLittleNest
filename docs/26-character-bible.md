# Purpose

Characters are one of the most important parts of The Little Nest experience.

They are not decorations.

They are emotional guides.

They help children learn.

They help parents understand.

They create delight.

They celebrate growth.

Every character should reinforce the values and philosophy of The Little Nest.

---

# Why Characters Exist

Characters exist to make learning feel welcoming.

They reduce anxiety.

They create smiles.

They explain difficult ideas simply.

They celebrate progress.

They encourage curiosity.

They help transform software into an experience.

They connect the classroom, the home, and the parent app into one continuous Little Nest world.

They welcome families back into that world every time they return.

They help transform each visit into a meaningful experience rather than simply another interaction with software.

---

# Character Principles

Every character should:

Teach.

Guide.

Encourage.

Celebrate.

Comfort.

Create wonder.

Reward curiosity.

Strengthen relationships.

Characters should never exist simply because they look cute.

Every appearance should have purpose.

# Tita Mayumi

## Role

Teacher.

Guide.

Mentor.

Storyteller.

Encourager.

Trusted adult.

---

## Personality

Patient.

Wise.

Kind.

Gentle.

Encouraging.

Optimistic.

Observant.

Calm.

Warm.

---

## Tita Mayumi Should

Celebrate effort.

Notice growth.

Explain patiently.

Encourage curiosity.

Help parents understand.

Support children.

Create confidence.

Protect childhood.

Help children feel safe.

---

## Tita Mayumi Should Never

Embarrass.

Compare children.

Rush children.

Create fear.

Use shame.

Be sarcastic.

Make children feel they have failed.

Become the centre of attention.

The child is always the hero.

---

## How Tita Mayumi Speaks

She speaks calmly.

She speaks kindly.

She asks questions more often than she gives answers.

She encourages instead of correcting.

She guides instead of instructing.

She never raises her voice.

She celebrates effort as much as achievement.

---

# Pico

## Role

Explorer.

Curious child.

Adventure companion.

Learner.

The child children see themselves in.

---

## Personality

Energetic.

Curious.

Funny.

Playful.

Imaginative.

Sometimes distracted.

Always learning.

Excited.

Kind-hearted.

---

## Pico Should

Ask questions.

Discover surprises.

Celebrate with children.

Model curiosity.

Make mistakes.

Learn from those mistakes.

Encourage exploration.

Learn good manners.

Learn kindness.

Learn patience.

Learn confidence.

---

## Pico Should Never

Ridicule.

Mock.

Create fear.

Steal important emotional moments.

Interrupt meaningful celebrations.

Know all the answers.

Become the teacher.

---

## Why Pico Exists

Pico is not the expert.

He is learning alongside the children.

When Pico learns to say "please"...

Children learn with him.

When Pico forgets to share...

Children see that mistakes are okay.

When Pico apologises...

Children learn empathy.

Pico gives children permission to be imperfect.

---

# Their Relationship

Tita Mayumi never embarrasses Pico.

She never says,

"You were wrong."

Instead she gently asks,

"What could we try next?"

Pico is comfortable making mistakes because he knows Tita Mayumi will help him learn.

Their relationship models the kind of relationship we want every teacher to build with every child.

---

# Characters and the Living Stage

Every time a family opens The Little Nest, the characters help welcome them into the next chapter of their child's story.

Tita Mayumi gently introduces meaningful moments.

Pico helps families discover them.

Sometimes they celebrate.

Sometimes they remember.

Sometimes they simply build excitement for tomorrow.

The characters should never force an experience.

They should invite families into it with warmth, curiosity, and kindness.

Like great hosts, they make every visit feel personal without becoming the centre of attention.

---

# The Classroom

Children should come to think of Tita Mayumi and Pico as familiar friends.

They should appear in the classroom.

In the Parent App.

In Story Reel.

In Memory Nest.

During celebrations.

During special events.

The characters create continuity between every Little Nest experience.

---

# Future Characters

Every new character should exist for a reason.

Characters should fill a meaningful role.

They should never duplicate another character's purpose.

---

# Writing Style

Characters should speak simply.

Warmly.

Positively.

Naturally.

Children should understand them.

Parents should appreciate them.

Humour should always feel kind.

Tita Mayumi speaks with gentle confidence.

Pico speaks with excitement and curiosity.

---

# Animation Philosophy

Movement should feel alive.

Not distracting.

Characters should breathe.

Blink.

Look around.

React.

Celebrate.

Wait patiently.

Characters should never move simply to attract attention.

Movement should always support the experience.

The smallest movement often creates the greatest emotional connection.

---

# Voice Philosophy

Silence is valuable.

Characters should not constantly speak.

When they do speak, their words should matter.

Simple phrases.

Gentle encouragement.

Celebrations.

Morning greetings.

Goodbye rituals.

Meaningful moments.

The less often they speak, the more meaningful their voices become.

---

# Emotional Goals

Characters should create:

Joy.

Wonder.

Curiosity.

Confidence.

Comfort.

Encouragement.

Pride.

Connection.

Hope.

Belonging.

---

# Character Hierarchy

The child is always the hero.

Parents experience the story.

Teachers create the moments.

Tita Mayumi guides.

Pico learns.

The characters help tell the story.

The characters should never become more important than the child whose story is being told.

---

# Final Principle

If removing a character makes the experience less meaningful, the character has purpose.

If removing a character changes nothing, the character should not be there.

Every appearance should answer one question:

"What experience does this character create?"
