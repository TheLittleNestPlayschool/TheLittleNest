# Purpose

The Main Stage is where families experience The Little Nest.

It is not a dashboard.

It is not a menu.

It is not a collection of features.

It is where the Living Stage presents meaningful experiences.

Parents should feel welcomed.

Not navigated.

---

# Philosophy

When parents open The Little Nest, they should immediately feel that something meaningful is waiting for them.

The application should never begin by asking:

"What would you like to do?"

Instead it should gently answer:

"Welcome back. We'd love to show you something."

---

# One Stage

Parents should feel they remain on one continuous stage.

Today's Chapter.

Story Reel.

Memory Nest.

Celebrations.

Learning Journey.

Gallery.

These should feel like scenes within one experience rather than separate parts of an application.

Transitions should feel natural.

The story should continue.

---

# The Hero Experience

Every visit begins with one featured experience.

The featured experience becomes the emotional centre of the stage.

Everything else quietly supports it.

Parents should never wonder what deserves their attention.

The stage should make that obvious.

---

# Supporting Experiences

After the featured experience, the Main Stage may gently offer:

Today's Story.

Story Reel.

Learning Journey.

Gallery.

Memory Nest.

Upcoming Adventures.

Celebrations.

Conversation Starters.

These should feel like invitations.

Never requirements.

---

# Characters

Tita Mayumi welcomes.

Guides.

Explains.

Celebrates.

Pico discovers.

Points.

Laughs.

Creates delight.

Characters should help experiences feel alive.

They should never distract from the child's story.

---

# Movement

The stage should always feel alive.

Characters breathe.

Blink.

Look around.

React.

Photos gently appear.

Cards slide naturally.

Celebrations unfold.

Nothing should feel rushed.

Nothing should feel static.

Movement should create warmth.

Never distraction.

---

# Freedom

Parents remain free to explore.

The Living Stage recommends a journey.

It never forces one.

Parents may:

Replay.

Share.

Open Memory Nest.

Explore the Learning Journey.

Watch Story Reel.

Return later.

Freedom creates confidence.

---

# Replay

Every meaningful experience should remain easy to experience again.

Parents often experience moments more than once.

First alone.

Later with their child.

Later with grandparents.

The Main Stage should naturally encourage replay without making it feel repetitive.

---

# Sharing

Families should first experience moments together.

Only then should sharing become a natural extension.

Sharing should never interrupt the experience.

It should follow it.

Parents should feel proud to share.

Never encouraged to advertise.

---

# Navigation

Navigation should quietly support the experience.

It should never become the experience.

Parents should always know where they are.

But they should rarely think about menus.

The story should remain the focus.

---

# Continuity

Every return should feel familiar.

Not identical.

The stage changes.

The child grows.

The story continues.

Parents should always feel they have returned to the same Little Nest.

Even though today's experience is new.

---

# Success

The Main Stage succeeds when parents:

Smile before tapping anything.

Feel welcomed.

Feel understood.

Immediately connect with their child's story.

Naturally continue exploring.

Replay experiences.

Share meaningful moments.

Return tomorrow looking forward to the next chapter.

---

# Future Evolution

The Main Stage should continue to evolve throughout the life of The Little Nest.

Future enhancements may include:

Seasonal themes.

Interactive celebrations.

Adaptive layouts.

Character interactions.

Family traditions.

Personalised welcome moments.

Ambient animation.

New experience types.

Every future enhancement should support the same goal:

Helping families experience childhood together.

---

# Final Principle

Parents should never feel that they are using an application.

They should feel that they have stepped into another chapter of their child's story.

If families close the Main Stage feeling closer to one another than when they opened it...

Then the Main Stage has fulfilled its purpose.
